Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Who is responsible

[NOMBRE LEGAL DEL TITULAR] is responsible for the data of Invento accounts. Contact: ineventocontrol09@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Account: email, password (stored securely by the authentication service), business name, business type, country, currency and language, and the version of these terms you accepted with its date.
  • Business data you enter: products, photos, costs, prices, inventory, movements, sales, receipts and reports.
  • Employees: name, email, permissions and the actions they perform in the app.
  • Credit customers: name and phone number, which the owner chooses to record.
  • Support: the messages and screenshots you send us.
  • Technical data: phone model, app version, errors and the identifier used to send notifications.
  • Subscription: the plan and whether it is active. Payments are processed by Google Play or Apple; we do not see or store your card details.
  • In Phase 2, for online-store customers: name, email and their orders.

3. What we use it for

To provide the service (accounts, inventory, sales, reports, backups), to charge and activate your plan, to send you alerts and notifications, to give you support and to keep the app secure. We do not use your business data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Who we share it with

Only with providers that make the app work: Google (Firebase and Google Cloud: authentication, database, file storage and notifications) and Google Play or Apple for payments. We may also share it if an authority requires it by law.

5. Where and how we protect it

Data is stored on cloud servers, travels encrypted, and each business can only see its own. Permissions are checked on the server. No measure is perfect, but we work to prevent unauthorized access.

6. How long we keep it

While your account is active. If you ask to delete it, the data is permanently erased as described in section 8. We may keep the minimum the law requires us to keep.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, and take it with you in a file (portability). Write to ineventocontrol09@gmail.com; many of these options will also be available in Settings once they are released in the app.

8. Deleting your data

  • To delete your account and data, write to ineventocontrol09@gmail.com from your account's email, as explained on the page "How to delete your account and data".
  • The account is deactivated once we confirm your identity, you have 7 days to change your mind, and then the data is permanently erased; server backups are erased within 30 days at most.
  • Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription: it must be cancelled in Google Play or Apple.

9. Data of your customers and employees

You decide what data about your customers and employees you store in the app and you are responsible for having their permission when the law requires it. We only store it on your behalf, for you.

10. Minors

The app is not directed to people under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app and ask you to accept it again.

12. Contact

To exercise your rights or resolve questions, write to ineventocontrol09@gmail.com.
