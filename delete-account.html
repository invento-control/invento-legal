How to delete your account and data

How to request deletion

  1. Write to ineventocontrol09@gmail.com from the same email you used to create your Invento account.
  2. Use the subject: "Delete my Invento account" and write your business name.
  3. We will reply to confirm it is you.

What happens next

  • Your account is deactivated once we confirm your identity.
  • You have 7 days to change your mind: if you write to us within that time, we reactivate it.
  • After that, your account and your business data (products, photos, sales, customers and employees) are permanently erased.
  • Server backups are erased within 30 days at most.

Important

Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. You must cancel it in Google Play or Apple.

If you want to keep your information, ask us for a copy of your data first.
